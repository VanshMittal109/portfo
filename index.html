About me

Hi, I'm vaibhavi rajvansh,

an animation student and digital artist with a background in watercolor painting and a growing passion for visual storytelling. I'm drawn to interesting characters, cinematic compositions, and the small details that make a scene feel alive. My work is inspired by nature, films, everyday moments, and the worlds that exist somewhere between observation and imagination.]

Alongside illustration and character design, I have a strong interest in storyboarding as a way of shaping narrative, emotion, and movement through visual sequences. I enjoy exploring how stories can be communicated clearly and effectively through images, whether it's a single illustration or a larger narrative project.

As an artist, I value curiosity, collaboration, and communication. I enjoy sharing ideas, and bringing creative concepts to life through thoughtful visual storytelling. This portfolio is a collection of my ongoing journey—experiments, studies, and projects that reflect both my growth and my aspirations as a storyteller and artist.
